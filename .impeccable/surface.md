# Operating surface

## Direction contract
THESIS: A warehouse collector centered on the current address and a short stream of accepted readings. No analytics dashboard as the main activity.

OWN-WORLD: White and cool mineral surfaces, deep green operational controls, dark camera glass. Crisp 1px separators, ample touch targets, system typography and monospaced business identifiers as specified in the brief.

STORY: Open a saved local survey or start a named one. Read address, read products, change location, export. The screen always explains the current association.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop uses compact navigation and a working column with camera at left and current address/recent records at right. Mobile brings the current address above the camera; primary scan control stays next to the feed. Signature interaction: a new address changes the large address field and emits a distinct brief status; successful products append visibly without a confirmation.

FORM: Industrial collector control panel. The user’s explicit industrial, minimal, operational visual requirements constrain the concept exercise (seed a4171343); code implementation follows that brief without an added approval cycle.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
